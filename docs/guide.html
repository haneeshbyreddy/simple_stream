Setup guide

Simple Stream

Show every project's screen in one Google Meet. Switch with one key.

Each device = a TV channel It waits quietly until you pick it.
Your laptop = the remote Press a number to change channel.
Meet shows one window Share it once, keep switching.

Set it up in 6 steps

1

Same Wi-Fi for everyone

All devices and your laptop join the same router or phone hotspot.

✓Jetson Nano ✓Jetson Xavier ✓Other laptops ✓Your laptop
Heads upSchool or office Wi-Fi often blocks devices from seeing each other. A phone hotspot always works.
2

On each device: paste one line

Open a terminal on the Jetson and paste this. Change the name to your project's name.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/haneeshbyreddy/simple_stream/main/client.sh | bash -s -- --name "Face detection"
Do it onceAfter this the device turns it on by itself every time it starts. Nothing to remember.
3

On your laptop: paste this line

This is the laptop you join the meeting from.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/haneeshbyreddy/simple_stream/main/server.sh | bash
Takes a minuteIt downloads a few things. Wait for the green ✔ installed.
4

Type simplestream

Two things open: a list of devices in the terminal, and a big window called Simple Stream.

$ simplestream
Nothing showing?Give it 5 seconds. Still empty? Jump to Uh-oh fixes.
5

In Meet: share the window

Click Present, pick A window, choose Simple Stream. Done. Leave it shared the whole time.

Uh-oh fixes

Device not in the list

Same Wi-Fi? Press r to look again. Or press a and type its IP.

Black picture

On the device's login screen, click the ⚙️ and pick Ubuntu on Xorg.

"No desktop session"

Someone must be logged in on the device. Turn on auto-login on the Jetsons.

Check a device

On the device, type simplestream-client. Green dot = all good.
